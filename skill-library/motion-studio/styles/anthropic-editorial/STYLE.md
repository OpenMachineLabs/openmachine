# Anthropic Editorial — unofficial motion direction

A restrained, tactile visual language for technical stories. Inspired by selected public Anthropic engineering illustrations and editorial product communication; not an official style guide, endorsement, or brand reproduction.

## Governing idea
**Make the mechanism feel like an object you can understand.** Begin with a tangible artifact—a paper strip, cut edge, sentence fragment, ledger line, or query—then reveal the missing relationship. Prefer spatial evidence to decorative interface chrome. The frame should feel authored on a page and precise in motion, not like an animated dashboard.

## Composition
- One dominant object or relationship per beat. Negative space creates the pause before a reveal.
- Use asymmetry with a clear optical anchor. Marginal notes are evidence, not persistent UI furniture.
- Paper, ink, rules, annotation, and translucent overlays have distinct material logic.
- Reorganize the frame as the mechanism changes, but preserve object identity across transformations.
- Do not default to rounded cards, centered headlines, persistent footers, generic nodes, orbiting rings, or gradient orbs.

## Type
- Use installed system sans and serif fallbacks; bundle no proprietary fonts.
- Hierarchy comes from size, weight, line length, spacing and position, not animating every line.
- Keep copy concise. At 1920×1080, critical labels should generally be 30–36 px and major headings 64 px or larger; verify at reduced playback size.
- Hold results long enough to read. Preserve metric denominator and scope.

## Materials and semantic color
- Paper #F2EFE8: quiet editorial ground, not an automatic default for every shot.
- Ink #23231F: primary type and structure.
- Graphite #65645E: secondary explanation.
- Rust #B95438: question, ambiguity, unresolved fragment.
- Leaf #607C68: context or grounded state.
- Blue-grey #8BA0A8: retrieval/index operation.
- Rule #D2CEC4: hairlines and quiet boundaries.

Use color consistently by role; neutral space should dominate. Color is not the sole carrier of meaning. Avoid gradients, neon, glow, luminous edges and decorative shadows.

## Story and motion
Move from concrete example to mechanism to evidence to caveat. A reveal answers a specific question: which document, what missing context, where does it enter the index, what was measured? Favor precise translation, unfolding, and settling. Use deliberate cuts when they improve clarity. Keep sound restrained and purposeful.

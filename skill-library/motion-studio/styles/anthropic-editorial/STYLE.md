# Anthropic Editorial — unofficial technical motion direction

This is an original, unofficial motion system informed by publicly available Anthropic editorial work and the existing repository illustration Skill. It does not reproduce Anthropic brand artwork, imply endorsement, bundle its proprietary fonts, or claim to be the official design system.

## Primary rule: the subject supplies the shapes

Identify a *protagonist object* and its states before styling any title. For retrieval, the protagonist is a strip of document: part of an authored filing, detached and anonymous, given context, indexed two ways, and returned as a recognizable search result. The film is not a rotation of generic templates. The selected case inspired the following transferable grammar, not a prescribed set of seven shots.

## Materials / composition

- **Archive paper:** authored line rules, filing identifiers, inset excerpts, punched edges and page-number marks. The document must look specific enough to explain why provenance matters.
- **Cut excerpt:** keep a consistent quoted sentence, edge coloring and proportions across the separation, annotation, indexing and recovery beats.
- **Generated-context band:** sage-tinted header physically expands on that excerpt. The words remain visibly the same; the source relationship is new.
- **Semantic material:** several thin moving, hand-directed strands with dots; these are *illustrative geometry*, never observed vector data.
- **Lexical material:** token lines and register marks distinct from the semantic field; no invented precision or ranking scores.
- **Evidence:** a dark green, calibrated quantitative scene. A bar's length has a named scale; keep measured claims and their setup on screen.

Compose with focal hierarchy and changed camera distance. An archival close-up, a spatial tear, an unfolding source band, two materially different indexing fields, a search path and a dark evidence scene should feel like one work, not a slideshow. Avoid permanent page-number chrome, decorative ring arrows, identical cards and dense small print.

## Typography, color and reading

Use system Georgia for editorial display and system Arial for technical metadata (nothing bundled). At 1920×1080, core explanatory words are typically 29–36px or larger; major headlines 74–110px. Tiny document furniture is texture, not required reading. Review the important copy at a 960×540 playback size.

Source palette used by the pilot:
- Forest #1B2E28 / ink #20342E: authority, dark evidence and primary structure
- Paper #F4F1E9 / white #FFFEFA / rule #C9CDC3: page materials and boundaries
- Rust #CB6A47: missing context, question, exact-word path
- Sage #769687 / mint #DDEAE0: provenance, attachment and recovered relationships
- Blue #4E8191 / sky #89ADBB: semantic path
- Muted #69756E: secondary explanation

These are *roles*, not a compulsory palette for every future subject. Color never carries meaning alone.

## Motion and sound

Cut visibly, retain object identity, reveal a new relationship, then provide a reading hold. Animations have anticipation, travel and settling; small indexing signals can continue while the viewer reads. Use Remotion's frame-driven React/SVG directly. Do not invent a DSL.

Design audio as an authored sequence: a restrained tonal bed; soft cut, dock, split, retrieval and measurement accents; a deliberate fade. No narration is needed when all essential information is legible muted. Do not imply audio has been listened to when only its waveform or stream has been inspected.

## Second subject demonstration

The Nine Hours engineering case exercises this same material-first method
without recycling the Contextual Retrieval scenes. Its objects are engineering
context fragments, an actual Skill cartridge, a laptop test surface, a CUDA
processor, a remote rack, and a calibrated utilization graph. The 42-second
timeline proves that the style is a **decision process**, not a fixed visual
template. Its 43%→93% result is marked as author-reported in one internal case.

## Accuracy and art rights

Source and date every measured figure. Mark fictive documents, tokens and spatial fields as illustrative, not experimental outputs. The existing anthropic-style-illustrations Skill is the upstream research/illustration method; adapt meaningful SVG layers or construct new time-aware vector artwork, never animate its entire dense static image blindly. See references.md and the editable pilot.

# Selected visual evidence / transfer decisions

## Official sources

- [Anthropic, Introducing Contextual Retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/research/contextual-retrieval), September 19, 2024. Directly read its ACME SEC-filing example, preprocessing description, separate embeddings/BM25 and 1 − recall@20 results. Transfer: physical separation of an excerpt, added provenance before indexing, and a calibrated final comparison. Do not copy the article's static diagrams as video art.
- [Anthropic Engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering). An editorial discovery surface, not proof of one uniform design system. Transfer concise example-to-mechanism explanation and careful caveats.
- [Existing illustration Skill](https://github.com/OpenMachineLabs/openmachine/tree/main/skill-library/anthropic-style-illustrations). Consult its evidence-first SVG composition and illustration research process. The pilot builds its own layered React/SVG, without modifications to or redistribution of that Skill.

## User-provided comparison films (not bundled)

Three supplied 1920×1080 MP4s were inspected by multiple sampled frames and media metadata:

- **Bella TTS, 39 s / 60 fps:** a persistent waveform language passes through examples, candidates, pairwise preference and closing; lines, hierarchy and pacing are chosen for the topic. Transfer coherent motion material, not a copied waveform.
- **OM-v2, 38 s / 60 fps:** authored filing/skill artifacts, a laptop, chip and compute racks connect product processes with recognizably different scene compositions, including a strong dark quantitative opening. Transfer original object illustration and optical hierarchy, not branded shapes.
- **Open Machine 39 s / 30 fps:** a clear workflow with distinct research, machines, evaluation and result visuals; a dark instrumented result shot anchors its measured claims. Transfer topical specificity and evidence discipline; avoid copying interface diagrams and numerical product claims.

Their uploaded MP4s contained video streams but no audio streams. These observations support visual comparisons only, not sound design assumptions. The supplied media remain outside the repository. No underlying source code, licensed assets or motion footage were copied.

## What was rejected

The first Motion Studio candidate looked like repeated title slides and low-detail paper cards. A shared cream palette did not make it authored motion. The new pilot uses a single named excerpt across materially distinct archival, context, semantic, lexical and measurement states. Direction B is separately rendered in the pilot, then rejected because the search-field protagonist hides the lost-document mechanism. Neither rendering is an official Anthropic motion reference.

Review the actual completed clip with sound and at intended viewing size before judging acceptance.

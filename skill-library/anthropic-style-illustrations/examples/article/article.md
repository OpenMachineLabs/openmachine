# A reading guide to agent workflow vocabulary

This original guide uses the recovered metadata for Anthropic's real article
[Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents).
It is a complete input article for this example, not a reproduction or a claim
that the full source article was read during this offline run. Its audience is
an engineer choosing which workflow question to investigate next.

## Distinguish the questions

The source figure captions identify prompt chaining, routing, parallelization,
orchestrator-workers, and evaluator-optimizer workflows. These names provide a
useful reading map. They do not establish that one pattern is universally best.
A comparison can help the reader separate the questions before opening each
source section: sequence, selection, concurrency, delegation, and revision.

## Compare two reading paths

Routing invites a question about selection. Parallelization invites a question
about concurrent work. This is an original interpretation of the workflow names,
not a finding about the appearance of their source figures. Read the original
article for its conditions, examples, and implementation advice.

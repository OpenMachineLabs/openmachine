# Code of Conduct

Open Machine aims to maintain a respectful, technically constructive community
for people of different backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies
to project Issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-operated community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when reporting problems or reviewing work.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction and evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and explain constructive next steps.
- Protect other people's privacy, credentials, systems, and unpublished work.
- Disagree about implementation choices without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory slurs, intimidation, repeated personal
attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project
spaces to solicit unauthorized access to systems or accounts.

## Reporting and enforcement

For misconduct on GitHub, use the platform's reporting tools. For concerns
about project community spaces, contact a project moderator privately where
a private messaging route is available. If you cannot reach one privately,
request a moderator contact in a neutral message **without disclosing personal
or sensitive details publicly**. General community entry points are linked
from the [README](README.md).

Maintainers or moderators may remove content, issue warnings, restrict
participation, or ban accounts from project-operated spaces in response to
conduct violations. They will consider severity, impact, and recurring behavior,
and will make reasonable efforts to keep reports confidential, without promising
absolute confidentiality or a particular response time.

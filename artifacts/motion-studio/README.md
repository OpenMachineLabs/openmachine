# Motion Studio pilot / current review bundle

The production project, editable compositions and generated-sound script live under
skill-library/motion-studio/examples/pilot-film/. These are externally viewable
review copies from the same composition in PR #205, not tracked production caches.

- contextual-retrieval.mp4: revised 40 s / 1920x1080 / 30 fps film, stereo AAC.
- mechanism-preview.mp4: real 9 s moving section (film frames 310–579).
- poster.png: representative frame of context being attached to an excerpt.
- contact-sheet.jpg: nine sampled frames covering the complete film.
- direction-study.jpg: real source-rendered A/B art direction comparison.

The composition has original vector artwork and an original locally synthesized
soundtrack. No reference-film samples, branded graphics or proprietary fonts are
distributed. The contextual excerpt, lexical matches and field visualization are
illustrative. The 5.7% and 2.9% chart values are sourced from Anthropic's September
19, 2024 Contextual Retrieval article, for the reported 1 − recall@20 evaluation.

The MP4s were rendered and inspected for duration, frame rate, dimensions, audio
streams and sampled scene frames. Those checks alone do not establish full
normal-speed audio/video playback review or public editorial acceptance.

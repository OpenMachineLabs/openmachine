## Research and maintenance

Browse the actual [Survey / Research Issues](https://github.com/OpenMachineLabs/openmachine/issues?q=label%3Aresearch),
[Benchmarks](https://github.com/OpenMachineLabs/openmachine/issues?q=label%3Abenchmark)
and [Roadmaps](https://github.com/OpenMachineLabs/openmachine/issues?q=label%3Afeature).
Research is evidence, never implementation approval.

Keep full designs, results and approvals in their original Issues; do not create
empty numbered directories, duplicate documents or prewritten acceptance receipts.
Only necessary attachments belong in `spec/<issue-number>/`.
See [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the workflow.

## How it works

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/openmachine-flow.svg" width="1000" alt="You ask your coding agent; Open Machine connects it to your machines with the access you set; results come back to your agent." />
</p>

1. **You ask.** Tell your coding agent what you need, as you do today.
2. **Open Machine connects.** Your agent reaches the right machine through your
   account. Each machine keeps its own identity, and only your machines answer.
3. **Your machines do the work.** Results come back to your agent. Branches and
   pull requests go to GitHub through your account, with no tokens left on the
   machine.

## Connect all your machines

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/openmachine-machines.svg" width="1000" alt="Laptops, workstations, GPU machines and servers connected through Open Machine." />
</p>

Your laptop, your desk machine, the GPU box and the build server become one
workspace. Add a machine with the Open Machine app, turn it on, and your agent
can use it.

## Power under your control

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/openmachine-access.svg" width="1000" alt="Choose Full access, Selected projects or Read only for each machine." />
</p>

| Access | What your agent can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Full access** | Work as you do on that machine. |
| **Selected projects** | Change only the projects you pick. Keys, other apps' data and Open Machine itself stay out of reach. |
| **Read only** | Look around, build and test, without changing your files. |

Turn a machine off and your agent can no longer reach it. Remove it and it is
gone from your account.

## Get started

1. Download the Open Machine app from [openmachine.app](https://openmachine.app) (macOS).
2. Sign in and turn it on, on each machine you want to use.
3. Ask your coding agent to work on one of them.

## This repository

This is **Open Machine Core**, the open-source part of Open Machine that runs on
each of your machines. It starts your agent's work there and applies the access
you chose. The app installs and updates it for you. To build or change Core,
read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Security

Full access means your agent can do anything you can on that machine; choose it
only for machines where that is what you want. Selected projects and Read only
rely on the macOS sandbox. Report vulnerabilities privately through the
[Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
